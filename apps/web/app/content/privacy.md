## What we collect and why

### Browsing the site

When you visit a page, our hosting provider receives the technical details every website gets: your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser's user agent. We use them only to deliver the site and keep it secure. Our own server logs record the page and response, but not your IP address.

Images and 3D previews are served from our file storage at Cloudflare, which also sees your IP address when your browser loads them.

### Downloading a model

When you download a file, we record which model and file it was, when, and your account if you are signed in. We don't store your IP address. We store a one-way code made from it, mixed with a secret key and the date, so we can enforce the download limit (30 downloads an hour) without knowing who you are. The code changes every day and can't be turned back into your IP address. We erase it after 7 days.

We use the rest of the record for the download counts shown on the site. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the site, preventing abuse and showing which models are popular (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

### Your account

You don't need an account to browse or download free models. If you create one, we process your email address and, if you give them, your name and profile picture. Accounts are handled by our sign-in provider, Clerk. We keep a copy of your user ID, email address, name and account role in our own database.

We use this data to sign you in and to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We keep it until you delete your account, or ask us to. When the account is deleted, your downloads stay in the counts but are no longer linked to you.

### Statistics, only if you allow them {#analytics}

If you click "Allow" in the cookie banner, we use PostHog to collect anonymous usage statistics: which pages and models people view, what they click, which site they came from, and their type of browser and device. PostHog gives your browser a random ID in a cookie, so repeat visits can be counted. PostHog is set not to store your IP address. We don't link this data to your account, and we don't record your screen.

The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. We then remove PostHog's cookies from your browser. PostHog keeps the collected events for up to 12 months. We use PostHog's EU servers in Frankfurt.

## Cookies {#cookies}

- **`pesoprint_consent`**, set by us: remembers your answer to the cookie banner. Kept for 6 months.
- **`__session`, `__client_uat`** and similar names starting with `__client` or `__clerk`, set by Clerk: keep you signed in and protect the sign-in. Clerk sets how long they last.
- **`ph_…_posthog`**, set by PostHog only if you allow statistics: the random ID used to count visits. PostHog also keeps a copy in your browser's local storage. Kept for 1 year.

The first two are strictly necessary for the site to work, so they don't need consent. PostHog's cookie is only set after you click "Allow".
